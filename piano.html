Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own instrument?

It's recommended to have your own instrument for practice, but we can discuss options if this is a barrier to learning.

How long are lessons?

Standard lessons are 30, 45, or 60 minutes. We'll find the duration that works best for your learning style.

Can I reschedule lessons?

Yes! Life happens. Please provide as much notice as possible if you need to reschedule.

Do you offer group lessons?

Primarily individual lessons for personalized instruction, but inquire about options for small groups.
