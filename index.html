About Cadenza Music Therapy

Hello! I'm Lacey Friesen, Music Therapist

A cadenza is part of a song where a performer has the opportunity to stray away from rhythm, tempo, and tonality and to instead display their creativity and artistry—in essence, to show what they can do!

At Cadenza Music Therapy, my hope is to create a space for my clients and students to grow in confidence and self-expression (both musically and non-musically) and to foster that growth beyond limits so they can share who they are and what they are capable of.

Thank you for joining me on this journey! 🎵

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up for classes?

You can sign up using the form below, and we'll contact you within 24 hours to confirm your enrollment and discuss scheduling.

What if I have no music experience?

No experience necessary! Our sessions are designed for all levels. Music therapy is about the therapeutic benefit, not musical skill.

How long is each session?

Sessions typically range from 30-60 minutes depending on the program. We'll discuss the best duration for your needs.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for 24 hours notice for cancellations. Reach out to us if you need to reschedule.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes, we offer both in-person and virtual sessions to fit your lifestyle and needs.

How much do sessions cost?

Pricing varies based on session type and duration. Contact us for a personalized quote.
